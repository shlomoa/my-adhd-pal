3. DECISIONS MADE
   - Hardware: the repository's own watch (Galaxy Watch6 Classic) and phone (Galaxy A55); built-in sensor, no extra hardware.
   - Two sensor routes compared: r1 Health Services, r2 SensorManager wake-up sensor with foreground service and wake lock.
   - Two phone paths sent in parallel for the same readings: direct Bluetooth RFCOMM (proves Bluetooth) and the Data Layer.
   - Latency measured as recorded time to arrival; the statistic is p95; a state is judged from at least 20 readings.
   - Limits: goal 1 at most 500 ms, goal 3 under 1000 ms on the Bluetooth path only; the Data Layer is never substituted.
   - Clock offset between watch and phone: from the shortest-round-trip ping/pong of the whole run, uncertainty printed.
   - Control is tested by direct commands to the watch; 'power' means the sensor connection and the CPU wake lock.
   - Run length 40 s per phase (about 7 minutes in total) to respect the 10-minute limit.

4. CONSIDERATIONS NOT TAKEN
   - Samsung Health Sensor SDK (raw PPG, inter-beat intervals): not needed for pulse data, needs a Samsung login.
   - Bluetooth Low Energy (GATT) instead of classic RFCOMM; Data Layer ChannelClient.
   - Real hardware power draw (battery current): out of scope by decision.
   - Long-duration, motion, multi-person or multi-watch testing; battery-saver and always-on-display settings.
   - Control from the phone app (control is by direct commands on the watch, as decided).
